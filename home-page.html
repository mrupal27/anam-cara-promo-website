Story

Anam Cara is a 2.5D historical fantasy co-op game following teenage siblings Wilfred and Winifred through a world shaped by witch trials, paranoia and dark experimentation. Together they must uncover the truth behind their village and their own past.

Key Gameplay Features

  • Asymmetrical co-op with two clearly different player roles.
  • Sword combat for Wilfred and magical possession for Winifred.
  • Environmental puzzles that require both players to work together.
  • A narrative-driven historical fantasy world with hand-drawn art.
